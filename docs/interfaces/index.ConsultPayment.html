    Interface ConsultPayment

    What accepting / joining / booking a consult costs the patient — or what already paid for it. Read it BEFORE the patient acts, so one button can say "Yes & pay CA$100" instead of "Yes" followed by a surprise (docs/partner-api/guides/payments.md).

    Present in two cases only:

    • status: 'due' — the consult is still an offer the patient can take (async invited; a live video consult invited/waiting; a scheduled one not yet booked), its link has not expired, and the tenant charges for its scenario. The amount is exactly what the payment gate will ask for — the same (modality, mode) the gate prices, never a catalog guess.
    • status: 'paid' — a payment already stands behind this consult: paymentId names it, and amountCents/currency are what was paid. On an invite this means the patient paid and has not completed the action yet — repeating consent / join / book lets them in without a second charge (the payment is bound to the consult and adopted automatically).

    ABSENT means "show no price", never "free": the scenario is free, the consult is past the point of paying, or the price could not be read right now. The action itself stays authoritative either way — a priced action without a payment answers 402 payment_required.
