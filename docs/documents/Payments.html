    Payments — "Accept & pay" in one action

    When a clinic charges for a visit, the patient should press one button — "Yes & pay CA$100", "Join & pay CA$100", "Confirm 3:00 PM · Pay CA$100" — and the payment step should open right away. Once the platform confirms the payment, the consent, join or booking completes. This guide covers the pieces behind that button and three ways to build it:

    1. Your server relays REST calls,
    2. The browser patient SDK (@natzar/client/patient),
    3. The widget (<natzar-agent>, <natzar-async>, <natzar-telehealth>), which needs no code.

    Everything below assumes the getting-started setup.

    The rules that never bend

    • Only the platform says "paid". A payment becomes paid only when Stripe's signature-verified webhook reaches Natzar. Stripe.js's onComplete, a visit to your success URL and a payment id in a query string are not proof. Wait for paid: true on GET /v1/billing/payments/{id} before you treat a payment as done.
    • The action decides. Consent, join and book each check the payment inside the same write that opens the visit. Any price shown before that is for display only. An action without a payment on a priced scenario answers 402 payment_required, whatever your UI showed.
    • An unreadable price list is not a free one. If the price list cannot be read, the platform shows no price, starts no checkout (payments_unavailable) and lets no visit through (payment_invalid / payment_check_failed). It never treats the visit as free.

    What costs money, and when it is paid

    A tenant prices each scenario, a (modality, mode) pair. GET /v1/billing/catalog lists them (visits[], amountCents: 0 = free). Each scenario is paid at the step that opens the visit:

    Consult Scenario Paid at
    Async consult invite (invited) async · live (book for a mode: 'scheduled' thread) POST /v1/async-consults/{id}/consent (accept: true)
    On-demand video (mode: 'queue') telehealth · live the first POST /v1/telehealth-consults/{id}/join
    Scheduled video, not booked yet telehealth · book (in_person · book for an in-person visit) POST /v1/telehealth-consults/{id}/book

    A booked appointment was paid when it was booked, so its join is free. An active membership does not waive a visit fee.

    The price before the click — consult.payment

    Both consult resources, AsyncConsultResource and TelehealthConsultResource, carry an optional payment block ( ConsultPayment). You see it on GET /v1/patients/state, on the consult GETs and lists, and on every lifecycle response:

    {
      "consult": {
        "id": "thr_8d2…",
        "status": "invited",
        "payment": {"status": "due", "amountCents": 10000, "currency": "cad", "modality": "async", "mode": "live"}
      }
    }
    • status: "due" means the patient can still take the offer: an async invite, a live video consult invited/waiting, or a scheduled consult not booked yet. Its link has not expired and the tenant charges for it. The amount is exactly what the action's gate will ask for. Label the button with it: new Intl.NumberFormat(locale, {style: 'currency', currency: currency.toUpperCase()}).format(amountCents / 100).
    • status: "paid" with paymentId means a payment already covers the consult. On an invite, the patient paid and has not completed the action yet. Repeat the action: they are let in with no second charge.
    • Absent means no price to show. The scenario may be free, the consult may be past the paying step, or the price could not be read right now. Absent never means "free". Render the plain button, and the action still answers 402 if payment turns out to be needed.

    An async invite that would fold into the patient's already-open consult shows no due: accepting it joins the open consult at no charge.

    Headless helpers carry the same price. resolveLinkActions() puts payment: {amountCents, currency} on a due consult link, and @natzar/client/patient puts it on TimelineEntry.link.payment.

    Checkout keyed on the consult

    POST /v1/billing/visit-checkout can name the consult instead of a scenario (tenant key only):

    curl -s -X POST "$NATZAR_API/v1/billing/visit-checkout" \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $NATZAR_KEY" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
      -d '{"asyncConsultId": "thr_8d2…", "uiMode": "embedded"}'
    # or {"telehealthConsultId": "…"}; the original {"patientId", "modality", "mode"} body still works

    The platform loads the consult (it must be yours, on a patient you provisioned) and works out the scenario itself. It then binds the checkout to that consult:

    • A repeat call reuses the checkout. A double click, a remount or a "Pay" retry after the patient backed out gets the checkout already in progress (reused: true) instead of a second payable session.
    • An already-paid consult answers alreadyPaid: true with its paymentId. settling: true means the patient finished Stripe's form but the webhook has not landed yet: wait for paid, then act.
    • A consult that can no longer be taken is refused, and nothing is charged. It is 409 thread_not_active when the consult is not invited any more, is already booked, or its link expired (details.status: "expired").
    • An invite that would fold answers free: true. Accept it directly.
    • The action adopts the payment. Once paid, the consent, join or book for that consult goes through without a paymentId, because the platform finds the payment bound to the consult. You never have to carry a payment id across a reload or a crash.

    The response is always HTTP 200 once the consult resolved. Read ok and the fields that are set ( BillingCheckoutResponse):

    Answer Fields
    Pay ok: true, paymentId, clientSecret + publishableKey (embedded) or checkoutUrl (hosted), reused?, consultId
    Nothing owed ok: true, free: true, paymentId: null
    Already paid ok: true, alreadyPaid: true, paymentId, settling?
    No checkout ok: false, error: 'payments_unavailable' | 'scenario_not_priced' | 'stripe_error' (retry stripe_error)

    A bound checkout session stays payable for 30 minutes (Stripe's minimum), so a pending payment is settled one way or the other quickly.

    Embedded or hosted

    • uiMode: "embedded" returns a clientSecret and the publishableKey of the Stripe account that minted it. Mount Stripe's form in your own page. No redirect happens.
    • uiMode: "hosted" (the default) returns a checkoutUrl to send the patient to. Custom successUrl / cancelUrl must use one of your partner app's allowed origins. The platform appends natzar_payment=<id> to them. Landing on the success URL proves nothing; check the payment first.

    Mounting Stripe.js

    import {loadStripe} from '@stripe/stripe-js';

    const stripe = await loadStripe(publishableKey);
    const form = await stripe!.createEmbeddedCheckoutPage({
      clientSecret,
      // The patient finished the form. This is NOT proof of payment:
      // wait for the platform's `paid`, then complete the action.
      onComplete: () => confirmPaid(paymentId),
    });
    form.mount('#payment');

    Waiting for paid

    Poll GET /v1/billing/payments/{id} until paid is true. The webhook usually lands within seconds. The response also names what the payment is for: consultId, modality, mode and paidAt. With the SDK:

    await natzar.billing.waitForPayment(paymentId);   // resolves when paid; rejects payment_invalid on failed/expired/timeout

    Recipe 1 — REST, your server relays

    Your server holds the key. Your browser shows the button and Stripe's form. Ask the action first, before starting any checkout. The platform checks expiry, state and the fold before it asks for money, so an invite that cannot be taken, or is free to take, is never charged for.

    // server: the patient pressed "Yes & pay CA$100"
    async function acceptInvite(consultId: string) {
      try {
        await natzar.asyncConsults.consent(consultId, {accept: true});
        return {done: true};                                        // free, folded, or already paid (adopted)
      } catch (e) {
        if (!isErrorCode(e, 'payment_required') && !isErrorCode(e, 'payment_invalid')) throw e;
      }
      const checkout = await natzar.billing.visitCheckout({asyncConsultId: consultId, uiMode: 'embedded'});
      if (!checkout.ok) throw new Error(checkout.error);           // payments_unavailable / stripe_error…
      if (checkout.free) return acceptInvite(consultId);
      if (checkout.alreadyPaid) return {waitFor: checkout.paymentId};
      return {paymentId: checkout.paymentId, clientSecret: checkout.clientSecret, publishableKey: checkout.publishableKey};
    }

    // server: the browser's onComplete (or a retry after a reload)
    async function completeInvite(consultId: string, paymentId: string) {
      await natzar.billing.waitForPayment(paymentId);
      await natzar.asyncConsults.consent(consultId, {accept: true});  // paymentId optional: the bound payment is adopted
    }

    The same shape works for the two video steps:

    • Live video: telehealth.join(id) → 402 → visitCheckout({telehealthConsultId: id, uiMode: 'embedded'}) → mount → waitForPayment → telehealth.join(id) again. Later heartbeats need nothing.
    • Booking: the patient picks a time, then telehealth.book(id, {startsAt}) → 402 → visitCheckout({telehealthConsultId: id, …}) → mount → waitForPayment → telehealth.book(id, {startsAt}) again. A booking invite whose window lapsed is refused up front — /slots, /book and the checkout all answer 409 thread_not_active (details.status: "expired") — so the recipe never starts a payment for it.

    When the patient says "Not now"

    A decline (asyncConsults.consent(id, {accept: false})) is refused while money is in play for the invite, so a payment is never left buying nothing:

    Answer When What to do
    409 thread_not_active, details.reason: "payment_settled" The invite is already paid for. Paying is consenting. Accept it instead (accept: true, no paymentId needed). If the patient really wants out, the clinic refunds from Stripe.
    402 payment_invalid, details.reason: "payment_not_completed" A checkout for the invite is still payable: Stripe's form may be open on another screen, or it just completed and the platform has not confirmed it. Carries the amount and scenario. Let it settle. Once paid, accept. A checkout that was opened and abandoned keeps refusing the decline until it lapses (about half an hour); an invite sent in a conversation usually expires first.
    402 payment_invalid, details.reason: "payment_check_failed" The platform could not check whether the invite was paid for. The invite is left as it was. Retry the decline.

    In every case the invite is left invited. Hide "Not now" while your payment form is open or a payment is being confirmed: a decline cannot go through then.

    Recipe 2 — the patient SDK (@natzar/client/patient)

    In the browser, on the patient session your server minted. There is no API key. care.acceptAndPay runs the whole round from one press. It asks the action with checkout: true, mounts the checkout the refusal carries, waits for the platform's paid, and repeats the action with the payment:

    import {connectPatient} from '@natzar/client/patient';
    import {loadStripe} from '@stripe/stripe-js';

    const care = connectPatient(session);

    function label(entry: TimelineEntry) {
      const price = entry.link?.payment;
      if (!price) return entry.awaiting === 'join' ? 'Join' : 'Yes';
      const amount = new Intl.NumberFormat(locale, {style: 'currency', currency: price.currency.toUpperCase()})
        .format(price.amountCents / 100);
      return entry.awaiting === 'join' ? `Join & pay ${amount}` : `Yes & pay ${amount}`;
    }

    async function onPress(entry: TimelineEntry) {
      await care.acceptAndPay(entry, {
        // Show Stripe's form, and resolve when the patient completed it.
        // Reject to abandon: the next press resumes the same checkout.
        mount: async ({clientSecret, publishableKey}) => {
          const stripe = await loadStripe(publishableKey ?? MY_PUBLISHABLE_KEY);   // same Stripe account
          let completed!: () => void;
          const done = new Promise<void>((resolve) => (completed = resolve));
          const form = await stripe!.createEmbeddedCheckoutPage({clientSecret: clientSecret!, onComplete: () => completed()});
          form.mount('#payment');
          try {
            await Promise.race([done, backedOut()]);   // backedOut(): rejects when your back button is pressed
          } finally {
            form.destroy();
          }
        },
        // For a `book` entry, pass the chosen slot: booking: {startsAt, timezone}
      });
    }

    The building blocks are also available on their own:

    • care.respond(entry, true, {checkout: true}) / care.telehealth.join(id, {checkout: true}) / care.telehealth.book(id, {startsAt, checkout: true}). On a payment refusal, checkoutOf(error) returns the bound checkout.
    • {paymentId} on the same calls presents a payment you hold.
    • care.payments.status(paymentId) returns the platform's paid.
    • BookingSlots.payment puts the booking price on the slot grid, for a "Confirm 3:00 PM · Pay CA$100" button.

    Recipe 3 — the widget (zero code)

    <natzar-agent>, <natzar-async> and <natzar-telehealth> do all of this themselves. The invite card reads "Yes & pay CA$100" (or "Join & pay …", or "Confirm 3:00 PM · Pay …" on the slot grid). The first press opens Stripe's form as a dialog over your page, and the visit completes after the platform confirms the payment. You add nothing; natzar:payment events tell you when the step opens and closes. See The payment step.

    402 reference

    Code details.reason When What to do
    payment_required — Priced scenario, no payment presented and none bound to the consult. details: amountCents, currency, modality, mode. Start a checkout (preferably keyed on the consult), wait for paid, repeat.
    payment_invalid payment_not_completed The presented payment is not paid yet, or none was presented and a checkout bound to the consult is still in progress. Carries the amount and scenario. Don't start another checkout. Finish or resume the one in progress (visit-checkout for the same consult hands it back), wait for paid, repeat.
    payment_invalid payment_mismatch The payment belongs to another patient, tenant or scenario. Use the right payment, or check out for this consult.
    payment_invalid payment_already_used The payment already bought a different visit. Check out again.
    payment_invalid payment_check_failed The payment or the price list could not be verified. Retry. Nothing was created.

    A decline can also draw payment_not_completed and payment_check_failed, and a decline of a paid invite is a 409 — see When the patient says "Not now".

    Edge cases

    • The 10-minute invite window. Invites sent in a conversation are valid for 10 minutes. A bound checkout can only be started while the invite is live, so a paid payment bound to an invite keeps it acceptable (and a live video consult joinable) after the window closes. The patient who spent the ten minutes on the card form is let in. Without a payment, an expired invite is refused, and the checkout for it is refused too.
    • The booking window. A booking invite a physician or the agent sent has three days to choose a time. Past that, /slots, /book and its checkout all answer 409 thread_not_active (details.status: "expired"), unless the patient already paid for it — then it stays bookable and the window restarts. Consults you created carry no window.
    • Fold. A patient who already has an open consult is folded into it on accept, for free. Such an invite shows no due, and a consult-keyed checkout for it answers free: true.
    • Slot lost after paying. The slot is not held during checkout. If it goes meanwhile, book answers 409 slot_taken with a fresh grid. The payment stays bound to the consult: book another time, with or without its id.
    • Double click / remount. The second visit-checkout for the same consult (or the second checkout: true press) hands back the checkout in progress. Two truly simultaneous calls can each mint a session. Show the newer one; the other dies unpaid when its 30-minute session expires.
    • The patient backs out. Nothing is charged, and the invite stays open until its window closes. The next press resumes the same session while it lives. The session is still payable meanwhile, so a decline of an async invite is refused (payment_not_completed) until it lapses.
    • The patient paid, then closed the tab. The payment is bound to the consult. The next press completes the action with no second charge (consult.payment.status reads paid meanwhile). The invite is not timed out while a payment for it may still land.
    • Declining after paying. A paid invite cannot be declined (409 thread_not_active, details.reason: "payment_settled"), and one whose checkout is still in progress cannot be declined until it settles (402 payment_invalid, payment_not_completed). See When the patient says "Not now".
    • An unused payment. A payment that ends up buying nothing (the invite folded into another consult, or the clinic cancelled it, after paying) stays paid and unspent in GET /v1/billing/payments. Refunds are the clinic's decision, made from Stripe. The API issues none automatically.

    Behaviour change: the REST join is payment-gated

    POST /v1/telehealth-consults/{id}/join now runs the same payment gate as the widget and the app. On a tenant that charges for on-demand video, the first join of an unpaid consult answers 402 payment_required and queues nobody. Before this change it queued the patient for free. Pay (Recipe 1), then join again. The join body takes an optional paymentId, and a payment bound to the consult is adopted without it. Tenants that do not charge for on-demand video see no change.
