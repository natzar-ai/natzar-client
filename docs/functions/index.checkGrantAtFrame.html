      The frame's check of a grant before it unseals (§4.3 step 6): protocol 2; its own environment, zone and partner; its audience and the bound surface; a sealed credential only; parentOrigin === boundOrigin; its own URL; its own apiUrl and graphqlUrl; the current session id on renewal. Then the frame unseals with the binding tuple rebuilt from those same facts, so a field the relaying page rewrote fails to open (RG0 F1).
